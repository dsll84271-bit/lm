# Ifterwards rulebook

Every episode follows these rules. Do not change this file without asking Leo.

## The channel

- Animated "What if you..." stories told in second person. The viewer is "you".
- One human-scale change per episode, followed through Day 1, Week 1, Month 1 and Year 1.
- General audience. Any age can watch, but it is not a kids' channel: grown-up stories, muted colours, no baby-ish designs or sounds.
- Every episode is 8 minutes or longer.

## Format

- 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 frames a second.
- Narration drives everything. Shots are timed to the voiceover file.

## World

- 3D sets with real depth: a foreground, a middle and a background in every shot.
- Flat 2D characters placed inside the 3D sets.
- Background slightly blurred, characters sharp.
- A new set for every location. Never reuse a set from an earlier episode unchanged.

## Camera

- Always moving, slowly. Push in on emotion, pull out on scale.
- No static shot longer than 4 seconds.

## The stickman

- Cream round head (#FFF4E0) with a thick dark outline (#0B1437).
- Two simple eyes (white with a dark pupil) and two short brows. Brows and mouth carry the acting.
- Stick limbs: cream with a dark outline, rounded ends, so he reads on dark and light backgrounds.
- One ownable detail: an amber scarf (#FFB703) that trails when he moves. It is the only bright colour on him.
- No name, no hair, no clothes, no voice of his own.
- Never off-model. Build the rig once and reuse it.
- Expressions to build first: neutral, delighted, shocked, suspicious, crushed.
- Poses to build first: stand, walk, sit, point, slump, hand to ear.

## Other people

- Same stick build, blue-grey heads (#9FB0D0), no scarf.
- At least one new supporting character each episode.

## Day cards

- A calendar page flips to DAY 1, WEEK 1, MONTH 1, YEAR 1 (and others when a story needs them).
- Same animation and paper-flip sound every time. This is the channel's signature.

## On-screen text

- Five words at most, cream, Fredoka font in its heaviest weight.
- One number on screen at a time.
- Never the narration word for word.

## Colours

| Role | Colour |
| --- | --- |
| Background | Night navy #0B1437 |
| The stickman | Cream #FFF4E0 |
| His scarf, numbers | Amber #FFB703 |
| Key words | Signal red #FF4D3D |
| Accent | Teal #19C2B1 |

Colour arc across an episode: Day 1 warm and saturated, Month 1 cooler, the turn almost monochrome, Year 1 warm again.

## Pacing

- The premise is on screen by 0:10 and the stakes by 0:30.
- Something changes on screen every 3 to 5 seconds.
- A new location at least every 30 seconds.

## Sound

- One signature sound per episode (the ring, in Episode 1).
- Whoosh on camera moves, paper flip on day cards.
- Music drops out completely for the turn.
- Only use sound and music that is free for commercial use, for example from the YouTube Audio Library or Openverse with the commercial-use filter on.

## Voice

- Kokoro, "Heart" voice, calm pace.
- Second person, present tense, short sentences.

## Story shape

Cold open, the rule, the advantage, the cost, the turn, Year 1, a closing question. Move the time jumps to fit each story.

## Hard rules

- Original characters only. No named heroes, anime powers, film footage or other channels' characters.
- Match the references' level of finish. Do not copy their characters, titles or scripts.
- Every episode has its own premise, its own locations and at least one real-world fact.

## From the references

Added 11 November 2026. Nothing above this line was changed.

**What was studied.** Five phone screenshots of three channel pages: IFAnime, Awaken and Jeremy Bearimy. That gives 7 thumbnails, their titles and their running times. The videos themselves have not been watched yet, so the rules below cover only what a thumbnail can show. Where a reference disagrees with a rule above, the rule above wins until Leo says otherwise.

### Measured so far

- **Length.** The seven reference videos run 8:03, 8:09, 8:14, 8:57, 10:32, 10:38 and 11:16. None is under 8:00. Aim for 8:15 to 11:00, so a slightly fast voiceover still clears 8 minutes.
- **Titles.** Six of the seven are a question that starts "What If You". They are 8 to 11 words long. Keep episode titles to 11 words or fewer.
- **Faces read at phone size.** In the four close-up thumbnails the main character's head fills more than half the frame height. In the full-body ones it is still about a quarter. In any shot that is about a feeling, the stickman's head is at least a third of the frame height.
- **Eyes.** On the two 2D references each eye is roughly a fifth to a quarter of the width of the head. The rig's eyes are 22% of the head width. Do not make them smaller.
- **Expressions are pushed.** Every thumbnail face is at an extreme: brows slanted hard, eyes very wide or shut, mouth fully open or a tight line. Nobody is neutral. Our thumbnails never use the neutral expression.
- **Finish on characters.** No reference character is a single flat colour. Each has at least one shadow tone and a highlight in the eye. The rig has one soft shadow along the lower edge of the head and one catch-light in each pupil. Keep both in every shot.
- **Depth in the picture.** The IFAnime thumbnails are built in three layers: something close to the camera, the character, and a room or landscape behind that is softer than the character. This matches the World rules above.
- **One dominant colour per picture.** Each thumbnail has one background colour that covers more than half the frame (orange, deep blue, pink, storm grey) and one contrasting colour for the text. Each shot has one dominant colour from the palette.
- **Thumbnail text.** One to three words, in capitals, in a heavy rounded typeface with a dark edge, filling roughly a quarter of the frame, in a corner away from the face. This is stricter than the five-word limit for on-screen text and applies to thumbnails only.
- **Supporting characters.** Two Awaken thumbnails show a crowd of five or more other people, and the lead can still be picked out at a glance. Ours is picked out by the cream head and the amber scarf, so nobody else ever wears amber.

### Where the references differ from this rulebook

These are choices for Leo. Nothing has been changed because of them.

- All three reference channels dress their characters (hoodies, suits, hair). The stickman has no hair and no clothes, only the scarf.
- The references use bright, saturated colour. This rulebook asks for muted colours.
- IFAnime's character is a shiny 3D model. Ours is flat 2D inside a 3D set.

### Still to measure from the videos

These need the video files. No numbers have been guessed.

- Sets: how many per episode, how much detail, how often a set is reused.
- Lighting: where the key light sits, how dark the shadows are, how colour changes across an episode.
- Camera: which moves are used, how fast, how long a shot holds.
- Cut rhythm: average shot length, and how it changes at emotional beats.
- Character animation: how many frames a pose change takes, how often they blink, how much they move while talking.
- On-screen text: size, position, how long it stays up.
- Sound: music level under the voice, how often sound effects land, where silence is used.
