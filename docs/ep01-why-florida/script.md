## Sources (checked 20 Nov 2026)

- **Leonida:** Rockstar's fictional Florida. Vice City, Grassrivers, Leonida Keys. [AV Club](https://www.avclub.com/grand-theft-auto-6-trailer), [Gematsu, trailer 2](https://www.gematsu.com/2025/05/grand-theft-auto-vi-second-trailer-characters-and-places-detailed)
- **The Great Freeze and Miami's founding:**
  - Freezes of 1894–95; Ingraham's orange blossoms (a legend, possibly a mixed bouquet); Miami incorporated 28 July 1896. [Vizcaya](https://vizcaya.org/beyond-vizcaya/the-great-freeze/), [PBS American Experience](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/miami-henry-flagler/), [Wikipedia: Julia Tuttle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Tuttle)
  - Voter counts vary by source (300–502), so the script says "a few hundred".
- **1926 hurricane:**
  - 18 Sept 1926; the eye passed over Miami; at least 372 deaths; the end of the land boom. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926_Miami_hurricane), [NWS](https://www.weather.gov/news/260915-great-miami-hurricane), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/americanexperience/features/miami-hurricane-1926)
  - Developers Fisher and Merrick ruined. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1926_Miami_hurricane)
- **Art Deco district:**
  - Listed 14 May 1979; 800+ buildings; the first urban 20th-century historic district on the National Register; Barbara Capitman. [Florida historical marker](https://markers.flheritage.com/home/details/16), [City of Miami Beach](https://miamibeachfl.gov/?p=308501)
  - Leonard Horowitz's pastel paint scheme. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/color-pallets-saving-buildings-from-demolition.htm)
- **Everglades:**
  - Douglas's *The Everglades: River of Grass* published 6 Nov 1947; Truman dedicated the park on 6 Dec 1947. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/ever/learn/historyculture/stoneman-douglas.htm), [Florida Memory](https://www.floridamemory.com/learn/exhibits/in-her-own-words/marjory-douglas/)
  - About 1.3 million alligators in Florida (FWC estimate). [ClickOrlando, Oct 2026](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/florida/2026/10/28/how-many-alligators-are-in-florida-the-state-once-counted/), [FWC](https://myfwc.com/wildlifehabitats/wildlife/alligator/facts/)
  - Pythons: contractors have removed more than 19,000 since 2017. [FWC, Python Challenge 2026](https://myfwc.com/news/all-news/python-challenge-0826/), [WUSF](https://www.wusf.org/environment/2026-08-07/nearly-300-pythons-are-removed-during-the-annual-challenge)
- **Florida Man and Chapter 119 public records:** [Reason](https://reason.com/2023/12/03/monkey-herpes-face-eating-and-the-pork-chop-gang-how-public-records-laws-created-the-florida-man/), [Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/news/how-floridas-proud-open-government-laws-lead-to-the-shame-of-florida-man-news-stories-7608595). The script includes the caveat that some experts think the law isn't the whole story.
- **Mariel and Miami's 1980s:**
  - About 125,000 people, April to October 1980. [ADST](https://adst.org/2015/04/a-flood-of-cuban-migrants-the-mariel-boatlift-april-october-1980/)
  - Time's "Paradise Lost?" cover, 1981. [Time archive](https://time.com/archive/6858774/miamis-agony/)
  - Wilbanks's estimate that the newcomers accounted for about a quarter of the rise in crime. [Caribbean Beat](https://www.caribbean-beat.com/issue-102/fidel-castro-score-one-maximum-leader)
- **Vice City's influences (Miami Vice, Scarface):** [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/5-times-gta-vice-city-referenced-movies-television), [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-vice-city-miami-beach-comparison-locations-music/)
- **Population:**
  - 1900: 528,542; 2020: 21,538,187 (US Census).
  - Florida passed New York in 2014. [Census Bureau release CB14-232](https://census.gov/newsroom/archives/2014-pr/cb14-232.html)
- **Sea level:**
  - Limestone "sponge"; sunny-day flooding during king tides; about 2 ft of rise expected by 2060; NOAA says flooding up to 50 days a year by 2050. [WLRN, 2023](https://www.wlrn.org/environment/2023-10-31/king-tide-floods-offer-glimpse-of-miamis-soggy-salty-future-can-anything-be-done)
  - Miami Beach pumps and road raising. [NRDC](https://www.nrdc.org/stories/can-miami-beach-hold-its-ground-against-king-tides)
