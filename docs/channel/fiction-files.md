Every story is built on something real.

Welcome to the Fiction Files. I'm your Detective. Every case file takes a film, series, anime or game and investigates the real history, people and places hiding behind it, with real archive evidence and every source listed.

Each case ends with a verdict: a Reality Score out of 10. How real is your favourite story?

New case files every week. Subscribe, and tell me which story to investigate next.
