Johnny Depp's Ebenezer is in cinemas, but the real men behind Scrooge were even worse. An MP who ate a bird a rat dragged out of a river. A banker whose coffin was pelted with stones. A miser who hid his fortune in a dung heap. Plus the gravestone that may have given Scrooge his name, and the real villain of A Christmas Carol.

Which real Scrooge was the worst? Tell me in the comments.
