# Pop-culture release calendar, mid-Nov 2026 to Dec 2027, with "real world behind the fiction" angles

Research date: 19 November 2026. Method note: WebFetch could not reach en.wikipedia.org, the-numbers.com or editorial.rottentomatoes.com from this session (DNS blocked by the session's network settings), so every date below comes from web-search result summaries of the linked pages, not from reading the full pages. The release-date sources are cited inline. The **real-world angles** are documentary pitches drawn from general knowledge; they were NOT individually sourced in this session and must be fact-checked before scripting (see Key Question 3).

Status key: **CONFIRMED** = studio/publisher/platform date; **REPORTED** = date from trade press or a single listing, not checked against the studio; **WINDOW** = only a year or season announced; **RUMOUR** = insider or unverified.

## Q1. Biggest films releasing Nov 2026 to Dec 2027 (with dates)

### Takeaway
The window opens with an unusually heavy run: Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (20 Nov 2026), then Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three on the same day (18 Dec 2026), then Jumanji: Open World (25 Dec). 2027 is stacked with family and franchise films: Narnia (12 Feb), Zelda (30 Apr), Star Wars: Starfighter (28 May), Spider-Verse (18 Jun), Shrek 5 (30 Jun), Man of Tomorrow (9 Jul), Minecraft 2 (23 Jul), Frozen III (24 Nov) and Avengers: Secret Wars (17 Dec). Several titles in the brief are **outside the window**. The Batman Part II has moved to 18 Feb 2028. Toy Story 5, The Mandalorian & Grogu, Spider-Man: Brand New Day and The Super Mario Galaxy Movie all came out before Nov 2026.

## Q2. Biggest games, anime seasons and TV series in the window (with dates)

### Takeaway
GTA VI comes out today (19 Nov 2026) on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S after two delays. It is the dominant gaming event of the window. The 2027 game slate is front-loaded: Stranger Than Heaven, Metro 2039, Tomb Raider, Persona 4 Revival and Fable in Jan-Feb, then FFVII part 3 in April and God of War in May. In anime, the clearest dated items are the Netflix/WIT Studio *The One Piece* remake (Feb 2027) and the One Piece Film *God Valley* (summer 2027). Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Part 2 is only rumoured for summer to Sept 2027.
