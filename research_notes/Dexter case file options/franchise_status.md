## Q1. Which Dexter series and seasons exist, and when did each release? What is the renewal status, and are there any 2026 announcements?

### Takeaway
There are four series: Dexter (Showtime, 2006-2013), Dexter: New Blood (Nov 2021 - Jan 2022), Dexter: Original Sin (Dec 2024 - Feb 2025, renewed and then cancelled in 2025) and Dexter: Resurrection (Season 1 July-Sept 2025; Season 2 streaming now, Oct 30 2026 - Jan 1 2027). The only 2026 Dexter release is Resurrection Season 2. I found no confirmed 2026 spin-off, film, Original Sin revival or Netflix deal, and Season 3 is not officially renewed.

## Q3. What are the key storylines and characters in the newest seasons that could anchor a "real story behind" angle?

### Takeaway
Resurrection moves Dexter (Michael C. Hall) to New York. In Season 1 he infiltrated billionaire Leon Prater's (Peter Dinklage) secret society of serial killers. Season 2 is set three years later and pits him against two killers: Brian Cox's "New York Ripper" (Don Framt) and Dan Stevens's "Five Borough Killer" (Owen Stark). Original Sin is set in 1991 Miami and shows a 20-year-old Dexter's first "Code" kills. The strongest real-world hooks are Dexter-inspired real crimes (Mark Twitchell in 2008; Benjamin Day, sentencing Dec 4, 2026) and the debate over whether a real vigilante killer inspired the character.

### Cited Findings

- Dexter goes to New York to find his son Harrison and repair their relationship.
