# The new "What if" channel

A faceless hero (the glossy orange head with the "?" hoodie) so every viewer pictures themselves. Built after studying the channels that get millions of views (Jeremy Bearimy, IFANIME, If Then Tales) with vidIQ.

## How an episode is made

1. **Topic:** checked with vidIQ keyword research first. Episode 1, "What If You Could Stop Time?", has about 322,000 searches a month and rising.
2. **Script** (`episodes/ep01/script.md`): one story about YOU in the present tense. Escalating scenes, a real number in every scene, a cost, a rescue at the climax, a twist, then "would you?" for the comments. Each `### name` is one scene.
3. **Narration:** `.venv/bin/python tools/voiceover.py episodes/ep01` (Kokoro "Heart" voice). Everything re-times itself.
4. **Scenes** (`episodes/ep01/scenes/`): each scene is a small world. The camera cuts between wide shots, close-ups and details on every sentence (about 20 cuts a minute, like the top channels), with slow pushes, tilted angles, the slowed-time look, labels, speech bubbles and bars.
5. **Render:** `bash tools/render.sh` (resumable chunks; joins them and sets the loudness).

## What is where

| Folder | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `characters/Hero.tsx` | The hero, and every other person (same body, different colours and hair) |
| `characters/sheet.png` | The model sheet |
| `components/stage.tsx` | Timing, the cut system, camera, letterbox, slowed-time look, snap ripple, sound |
| `components/ui.tsx` | Labels, speech bubbles, bars, big numbers |
| `sets/` | Streets, rain, the bus, cars, sports fields, rooms and furniture |
| `public/sfx`, `public/music` | Sound effects and two music beds, all generated by `tools/make_sounds.py`, free to use |

The voice tool and model are shared with the Ifterwards project; make `.venv` and `tools/models` links to it.

Thumbnails are made separately (in ChatGPT).
