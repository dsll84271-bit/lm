# Day 5: Dex Shrinks

Day One Hero. Every morning Dex wakes up with a new power, and we find out what it would really be like, with real science.
Day 4 was invisibility. Day 5 is shrinking.

How this file works (tools/voiceover.py reads it):
- Each `### name` is one shot. The shots play in this order.
- `NARRATOR:`, `DEX:` and `MUM:` lines are spoken. Dex's voice is pitched up, because a tiny voice box makes a high voice.
- `[hold 1.5]` adds that many seconds of picture after the shot's lines.
- `[pause 0.8]` adds silence before the next line.
- Lines in *italics* describe the picture and are not spoken.

Facts used, and where they come from:
- J. B. S. Haldane, "On Being the Right Size" (1926): a mouse dropped down a deep mine shaft "gets a slight shock and walks away"; "an insect going for a drink is in as great danger as a man leaning out over a precipice".
- Muscle strength depends on the muscle's cross-section (it shrinks with the square of size) while weight shrinks with the cube, so small bodies are strong for their size.
- Heat leaves through the skin. Smaller bodies have more skin for their weight, so they cool faster.
- Shrews eat roughly their own body weight in food every day (figures quoted range from about 80% to over 100%).
- Smaller vocal folds vibrate faster, so the voice is higher, and a tiny pair of lungs makes very little sound.
- To something very small, a liquid's surface tension is strong enough to trap it. Small insects can get stuck in a single drop.
