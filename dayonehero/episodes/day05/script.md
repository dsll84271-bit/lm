# Day 5: Dex Shrinks

Day One Hero. Every morning Dex wakes up with a new superpower, and we find out what it would really be like, with real science.
Day 4 was invisibility. Day 5 is shrinking. The episode runs 8 minutes or more.

Made the way Day 4 is made (measured from the Day 4 audio):
- One narrator, Kokoro's "Heart" voice, present tense, short sentences, real numbers.
- Characters never speak out loud. What they say is written on screen.
- The power's limits are counted as rules: "Rule one. Small means cold."
- The morning is the science. The evening is a story where Dex uses the power, limits and all, to help someone.

How this file works (tools/voiceover.py reads it):
- Each `### name` is one shot. `NARRATOR:` lines are spoken. `[hold 1.5]` adds seconds of picture after the lines, `[pause 0.8]` adds silence before the next line. Lines in *italics* describe the picture.

Facts used, and where they come from:
- One centimetre against a child of about 1.4 m: about 140 times shorter. A ladybird is about 5 to 8 mm long.
- Cutting a block into 8 smaller blocks doubles its outside surface; surface per gram goes up as size goes down, so small bodies lose heat faster.
- The Etruscan shrew weighs about 2 g and its heart can beat over 1,000 times a minute; a resting person's heart beats about 60 to 100 times.
- Shrews eat roughly their own body weight in food a day, need to eat every few hours, and can starve in well under a day.
- A house about 15 m long, scaled by 140, is about 2 km. A 60 cm stride scaled down is about 4 mm; at two steps a second, 15 m takes about half an hour.
- A grain of sugar (about 0.5 mm) scaled up by 140 is about 7 cm, the size of a tennis ball.
- Air resistance matters more the smaller and lighter you are; ants survive falls from any height. J. B. S. Haldane, "On Being the Right Size" (1926): a mouse dropped down a mine shaft "gets a slight shock and walks away"; "an insect going for a drink is in as great danger as a man leaning out over a precipice".
- Strength depends on a muscle's cross-section; weight on the whole body. Ants carry many times their own weight. A cornflake weighs a few hundredths of a gram, a few times Dex's weight (about a hundredth of a gram).
- The voice comes from vibrating vocal folds; smaller folds vibrate faster and sound higher. Human hearing tops out around 20,000 vibrations a second. Mice make many calls in ultrasound. Lungs scaled by 140 are under 2 mm.
- A 50 cm dog scaled by 140 is about 70 m, taller than a 20-storey building.
- Surface tension lets pond skaters stand on water and can trap small insects in a drop.
- Mum's pocket rising about 4 cm a step, scaled by 140, is about 5.6 m: two storeys.
- A gold ring weighs about 3 g, roughly 250 times Dex's weight; for a 35 kg child that is like 9 tonnes, more than an elephant.
